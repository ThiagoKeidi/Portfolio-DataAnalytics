# 🤖 AI-Powered Maintenance Intelligence Platform

## Overview

This project was developed to transform maintenance data into actionable business decisions by combining Power BI analytics with Generative Artificial Intelligence.

Traditional maintenance reports usually require supervisors and engineers to manually analyze dozens or hundreds of maintenance work orders, identify recurring failures, determine priorities, and create action plans.

The objective of this solution was to automate this process and accelerate decision-making within the maintenance department.

---

## Business Challenge

The maintenance department generates a large volume of operational data every month.

Although this information contains valuable insights, extracting meaningful conclusions often requires significant manual effort.

Challenges included:

- Identifying recurring failures
- Finding the machines generating the highest losses
- Prioritizing maintenance efforts
- Creating monthly performance reports
- Developing corrective and preventive action plans

The process was time-consuming and highly dependent on human analysis.

---

## Solution

A centralized Power BI platform was developed to consolidate maintenance history and operational performance indicators.

The solution analyzes all maintenance work orders and automatically highlights:

- Recurring failures
- Critical equipment groups
- Machines with the highest maintenance downtime
- Machines responsible for the largest production losses
- Maintenance performance trends

The platform was later integrated with Generative AI, allowing users to generate executive reports and action plans based on the analyzed data.

---

## Artificial Intelligence Integration

The AI integration was designed to support supervisors and engineers during the decision-making process.

Based on the maintenance indicators and historical maintenance data, the system can generate:

- Executive maintenance reports
- Monthly performance summaries
- Root cause analysis support
- Equipment prioritization recommendations
- Corrective action plans
- Preventive action plans

This significantly reduces the time required to transform operational data into improvement initiatives.

## Key Features

### Maintenance Performance Monitoring

Analysis of maintenance performance indicators across the entire operation.

### Asset Prioritization

Automatic identification of equipment and machine groups requiring immediate attention.

### Recurring Failure Detection

Identification of failure patterns and chronic maintenance issues.

### Production Loss Analysis

Monitoring of maintenance events with the highest operational impact.

### AI-Generated Reports

Automatic generation of structured maintenance reports.

### AI-Generated Action Plans

Creation of data-driven action plans focused on reducing recurrence and improving reliability.
