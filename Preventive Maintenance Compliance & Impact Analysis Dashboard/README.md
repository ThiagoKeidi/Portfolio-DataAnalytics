# Preventive Maintenance Control and Delivery Forecast Dashboard

## Overview

This Power BI solution was developed to monitor preventive maintenance execution, track monthly compliance performance, and forecast the likelihood of successfully completing the maintenance plan by the end of the month.

Beyond traditional preventive maintenance tracking, the dashboard provides proactive delivery forecasting and impact analysis, allowing maintenance teams to identify risks early and take corrective actions before compliance targets are compromised.

---

## Business Problem

Preventive maintenance activities are essential for ensuring equipment reliability and operational continuity.

However, maintenance teams often struggle to monitor the progress of preventive maintenance plans throughout the month and identify risks that may prevent completion before the monthly deadline.

Without clear visibility into execution performance, overdue maintenance tasks can accumulate, increasing operational risk and reducing maintenance plan compliance.

This dashboard was developed to centralize preventive maintenance monitoring, provide real-time execution visibility, and support proactive maintenance planning through delivery forecasting and impact analysis.
